# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define the quality strategy for a project and ensure features meet users' needs and business expectations before release. They partner with engineering, product, and project teams to validate the work against acceptance criteria and readiness standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain testing strategies across unit, integration, end-to-end, and regression coverage
- Review acceptance criteria and translate them into test plans and scenarios
- Validate that features meet quality standards before release
- Identify, document, and prioritize defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Advise teams on testability, automation opportunities, and quality risks

### Goals
- Improve product quality and user trust
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Create confidence in release readiness and delivery predictability

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning, backlog refinement, and test planning sessions
- PR and defect triage discussions with developers
- Quality status updates during project reviews and release readiness checkpoints

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to review feature quality, test coverage, and defect reproduction
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria, validation steps, and release confidence
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timelines, QA gates, and risk tracking
- Escalates issues to stakeholders and sponsors when risks affect business outcomes or launch readiness

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent the business, customer, or organizational interests behind a project. They set priorities, sponsor decisions, and help unblock work when cross-functional dependencies or decisions are needed.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation, scope, and major milestones
- Set business priorities, success measures, and financial or strategic direction
- Provide resources, sponsorship, and decision-making support
- Review progress and escalate issues that affect outcomes, risk, or delivery commitments
- Validate that solutions are aligned with business goals and customer value

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable value to the organization and customers
- Maintain alignment between portfolio priorities and team execution
- Enable fast decisions and appropriate support when blockers appear

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates, milestone reviews, and steering meetings
- Escalations for major risks, dependencies, or business-impacting issues
- Approval checkpoints for scope, funding, and release readiness

### Interaction with existing roles
- Sets priorities and success metrics with Product Managers
- Reviews project status and major risks with Project Managers
- Supports Product Managers and PMs in resolving cross-team dependencies and resource constraints
- Receives readiness updates from QA/Testing Leads and development teams before launch decisions

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers protect the organization and customers by ensuring projects meet security, privacy, and regulatory requirements. They guide secure design decisions and verify that releases are compliant before production.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements, threat models, and risk considerations for project work
- Advise on secure design patterns, data handling, and compliance requirements
- Participate in architecture and design reviews to identify risks early
- Validate that implemented features meet required controls, privacy, and regulatory obligations
- Support incident response and remediation planning when issues arise
- Maintain documentation needed for audit, governance, and policy alignment

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across project delivery
- Maintain trust, privacy, and customer protection
- Enable safe, policy-aligned release practices

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and security checkpoints
- Security audit findings, remediation plans, and compliance status updates
- Release and incident communications for risks that require escalation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Developers and Technical Architects to review implementation choices and risk mitigation
- Provides guidance to Product Managers and Project Managers on compliance impacts, risk posture, and release gating
- Works with Stakeholders/Sponsors on policy, approval, and enterprise risk concerns
- Supports QA/Testing Leads through security-focused validation and release readiness checks

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects shape the system design and technical strategy for a project. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards while balancing business needs and delivery constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define high-level system architecture, integration patterns, and technical direction
- Review designs for scalability, performance, maintainability, and reliability
- Set technical standards, guardrails, and best practices for the team
- Guide architecture trade-offs and mitigate technical risks early
- Support development teams with architectural decisions, design reviews, and technical mentoring
- Help align engineering decisions with broader platform and business goals

### Goals
- Deliver resilient, maintainable, and scalable solutions
- Reduce rework, architectural drift, and technical debt
- Align engineering decisions with long-term product and platform strategy

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews, technical design sessions, and platform discussions
- Technical feasibility assessments and decision logs
- Coordination with engineering leads on dependencies, standards, and risk mitigation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to review solutions and technical trade-offs
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers to ensure technical feasibility, sequencing, and roadmap alignment
- Supports QA/Testing Leads by influencing testability, observability, and production readiness considerations
- Advises Security & Compliance Officers on system structure, controls, and secure design patterns
